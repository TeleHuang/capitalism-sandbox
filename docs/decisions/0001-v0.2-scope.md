# ADR 0001 — V0.2 scope: a testable economic core, not the full PRD

- Status: accepted (provisional)
- Date: 2026-10-24
- Context: V0.1.1 could run 240 months but the industrial chain froze in month 2, the
  agricultural sector never transitioned, and there was no enterprise accounting or
  measurable profit rate.
- Decision: V0.2 implements a deterministic, testable monthly core:
  - 9 sectors and an explicit equipment catalogue;
  - a double auction with reference-price tatonnement and cost-based price bands;
  - a labour market with actual wage payments, hours and unemployment;
  - enterprise/owner cash separation, bank liquidity loans and dividends;
  - a land market and capitalist farms that buy machinery and fertilizer.
- Consequences:
  - LLM agents, contracts, futures, Web UI and scenarios 0/2 are deferred;
  - the model is intentionally a vertical slice; long-run calibration is not finished;
  - every later feature must update `03_架构设计文档.md` and the tests in the same change.
